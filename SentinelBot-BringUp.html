Work strictly top to bottom. Each stage assumes the one above it passed — that is the whole point of the ordering. A joystick that does nothing could be a dead motor driver, a wrong topic name, a silent JSON decode failure, or a stale emergency stop still sitting on the broker, and you cannot tell which until the layers below are known-good.

The wheels stay off the ground until Stage 6. Put the chassis on a book or a box so it can spin freely. Mark each item pass / fail / blocked; failures open a notes box — type what you saw and hit Copy report at the bottom to send the results back to me.
Checks are derived from the app's own contract: topic strings in Core/Utilities/Constants.swift, payload shapes in RobotCommand.swift and TelemetryPacket.swift, and QoS / timing rules in CommandService.swift. Progress is held in the page only — copy the report before closing.
